Kwent AI – Messenger Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you message the Kwent AI Facebook Page (facebook.com/kwentai) or a Facebook Page that uses our MSG-GROK service on Messenger, how we use it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how to ask us to delete it.

1. Who we are

  • Business: Kwent AI, operated by Kwent IT Solutions (DTI registration pending), based in the Philippines. Contact person: Alwin Reyes. Address: 11 San Vicente Ferrer, Brgy. San Antonio - Valley 1, Parañaque City, Philippines.
  • Messenger app: Kwent AI runs the Meta app "Kwent AI" (MSG-GROK), which sends automated Messenger replies for its own Page and for client Pages that subscribe to MSG-GROK.
  • Privacy contact: kwentoperations@gmail.com, or message our Facebook Page. This contact also handles data protection officer duties.

When MSG-GROK runs on a client's Page, that business decides why it uses Messenger and is responsible for its own customers' data; Kwent AI processes the messages on its behalf to provide the service.

2. What we collect

When you message a Page that uses our service, we collect only:

  • Your Page-scoped ID, a random ID Meta assigns for conversations with that Page only, used to send the reply to the right person.
  • Your message text and anything you choose to send in the chat (for example your business name, services you're interested in, or screenshots such as proof of payment).
  • Your name, if you give it in the chat or if it is provided by Meta with the conversation.
  • Details you share to sign up for a service, book a call, or follow up on an order (business name, Page link, phone number, email, preferred call time), only if you send them.
  • Basic technical records such as message timestamps and delivery status.

We do not collect your Facebook password, friends list, or anything else from your profile. We don't ask for payment card numbers in Messenger.

3. Why we use it

We use this information only to:

  • answer your questions (for Kwent AI: our services, pricing, onboarding, and booking a call);
  • pass your conversation to human staff when the automated assistant can't help;
  • handle your order or request if you ask us to;
  • keep a short record so we can follow up and fix problems.

We do not:

  • send marketing or promotional broadcasts through Messenger;
  • message you unless you messaged first; we only reply within Messenger's 24-hour reply window;
  • sell, rent, or trade your information;
  • use your messages for advertising or to build a profile of you;
  • use your messages to train our own AI models.

Legal basis (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, RA 10173): we process your information to respond to the request you make by messaging, to handle any order you place, and on our legitimate interest in providing customer service.

4. Automated replies

Replies may come from an automated AI assistant. It answers only from the Page's approved FAQ and says it is automated. If it can't answer, or you ask for a person (for example by typing "human"), it hands the conversation to staff. The assistant does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. How long we keep it

  • Conversation data held by us and our processors (automation logs, staff alerts, conversation records): up to 90 days after your last message, then deleted.
  • Order and billing records: as long as needed for the service, billing, and Philippine tax and accounting requirements.
  • Messages in the Page inbox are stored by Meta under Meta's policies. We delete the conversation from the Page inbox when you ask us to delete your data.
  • The AI model provider keeps message text only as long as its API terms allow.

7. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed; to access your information; to correct it; to object or withdraw consent; to delete or block it; to data portability; to be compensated for damages from wrongful processing; and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). Contact us as described in Section 8. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by sending the request from the same Messenger account.

8. How to delete your data

Send "delete my data" to the Page on Messenger, or email kwentoperations@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Full steps, timeline, and what is deleted: Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Security

We limit access to the team members who need it, use our providers' security protections, and keep access credentials private. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission when the law requires it.

10. Children

Our Messenger service is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date. For significant changes we will post a notice on our Page.
